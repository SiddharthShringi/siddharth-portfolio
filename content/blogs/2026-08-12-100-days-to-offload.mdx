This is #1 of my [100 Days to Offload challenge](https://100daystooffload.com/).
I came across this website and learned about this challenge.

The challenge is simple but not easy: publish 100 posts in a year.
It means that every month I need to publish 8-9 posts, or 2 posts every week.

## The challenge guidelines are simple:

- You need to post them on your own blog site.
- 100 posts can start and end whenever you want,
  the only condition is it should be completed within 365 days.
- No limits to what you can post, write about whatever interest you.
- You don't need to write long-form, deep, well written articles.
- Spelling and grammar mistakes are also fine.
- Even if there is no real point to the post,
  still the important thing is that you are writing
  about the things you want to write about.
- The point of this challenge is to **Just Write.**

## Why I'm doing this challenge:

- I always wanted my portfolio/blog where I can write about things that interest me.
  Now that I have one, I want to actually use it.
- I don't want my portfolio to be only about technical stuff,
  I want it to reflect the things I find interesting and want to remember.
- To push myself to write more and
  get comfortable expressing my thoughts without worrying about perfection.

That's the plan. Post #2 coming soon.
