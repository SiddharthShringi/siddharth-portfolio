This is Post #5 of 100 Days to Offload challenge.

When I work, I like having some background sounds.
It helps me stay focused.
Most of the time, I listen to background sounds like white noise,
brown noise, or lo-fi music.
One site I use is [mynoise.net](https://mynoise.net/).
It has a vast collection of background sounds for various purposes,
and I really like it.

It also has a built-in timer that I use for working sessions.
There is also a meditation bell feature, which I find helpful.
