A simple way to share what I'm currently focusing on and keep track of my priorities.

I came across this concept of [Now Page](https://nownownow.com/about)
through an [article](https://sive.rs/nowff) by Derek Sivers.
I like the idea of having a now page on a portfolio,
which tells visitors what this person is currently focusing on.
Having a now page also serves as a reminder of current priorities for a person.

It is a nice addition to a personal website where visitors can get to know more about you.

There are so many profiles listed on [here](https://nownownow.com),
where people share what they are currently working on.

I took some inspiration from these profiles and
created a now page for my portfolio.
I also finally added my portfolio's
Now page on [nownownow.com](https://nownownow.com/p/1JDt).

If you have a personal website,
I would encourage you to consider adding a Now page.
