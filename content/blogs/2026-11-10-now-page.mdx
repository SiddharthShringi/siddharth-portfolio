I created now page after coming across this [article](https://nownownow.com/about) by Derek Sivers. I wanted to have a page where I can share what I am doing right now.
This is a live page that shows my current activities.

Also having a now page works as a reminder to my current priorities.
It helps me to stay focused on what I am doing right now and not get distracted by other things.

I would also encourage if you don't have now page on your portfolio, you must add it.
